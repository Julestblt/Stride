# Progress measures

The Progression destination reads normalized garmin_mcp rows (`provider_activities`, `lap` rows of `provider_activity_segments`, `provider_activity_tracks` and `provider_observations`), the athlete's races and the active plan. `src/server/progress` loads them; pure functions in `src/features/progress` compute every value. Dates are the activity's local day; weeks start on Monday.

Progression has three sections: Forme (`/progress`), Corps (`/progress/body`) and Records (`/progress/records`). Each page has one server loader that starts its reads in parallel (`loadFitness`, `loadBody`, `loadRecords`) and passes plain data to pure view builders. Every insight leads to the plan, the source activities or a prefilled Coach request: action links open `/coach/nouvelle?texte=…` (trimmed, at most 500 characters, with an optional `seance` attachment) and never create a proposal. The old `/progress/recovery` and `/progress/health` URLs redirect permanently to Corps and keep `view` and `date`.

## Shared inputs

- Garmin race predictions: `race_prediction_5k_seconds`, `race_prediction_10k_seconds`, `race_prediction_half_marathon_seconds` and `race_prediction_marathon_seconds` over the last 120 days (`readPredictions`, shared with the Coach athlete context). The latest value is shown with its change against the latest value at least 28 days older, so Progression shows the same figures as the Coach. The Daniels–Gilbert VO₂ functions in `predictions.ts` remain only for plan targets.
- Goal race: the active plan's primary race when it is upcoming, otherwise the next upcoming A or B race (priority, then date). Without one, Forme shows an empty state linking to Courses.
- Plan phase: the current plan week number of the plan's total weeks and its block, from `buildProgram`; the taper date is the first week of the `taper` block. Without an active plan there is no phase bar, no planned distance and no consistency value.

## Forme

The period selector (6 weeks, 12 weeks, 6 months by default, 1 year, kept in the URL) applies to the condition chart and the measure rows. Trends compare a current window ending today with a baseline window starting at the first sample inside the period; both last a quarter of the period, clamped to 14–28 days, must not overlap and need three samples. Without a baseline a row shows "Tendance à confirmer"; without a current value it shows an unavailable state, never zero.

## Corps

Corps has three scales: 14 jours (default), 30 jours (`?view=month`) and Jour (`?view=day&date=YYYY-MM-DD`). The 14- and 30-day scales read stored daily observations; a measure is "today's" only when observed today or yesterday.

### Jour

`/progress/body?view=day&date=YYYY-MM-DD` (today by default, up to two years back) reads the day and the previous day from garmin_mcp's `GET /v1/wellness/days/{date}`, fetched on demand and never stored, plus the day's activities and the stored daily observations for reference ranges. The window runs from 22:00 the previous evening to 22:00, Europe/Paris time; the future part of today is hatched and never drawn.

- Indicators: sleep (phases sum, bedtime, wake time, awakenings), the night's HRV against the usual range, resting HR against its median, latest Body Battery against wake-up, average stress with its longest high episode, and steps against the Garmin goal.
- Timeline: sleep phases, heart rate (2-minute averages) with resting HR, stress (3-minute bars coloured by Garmin category), Body Battery and hourly steps. Ten-minute slots feed the inspector.
- Moments: bedtime, night awakenings of 5 minutes or more, wake-up with Body Battery and overnight recharge, recorded activities, high-stress episodes (above 50 for at least 30 minutes while awake) and the longest calm pause (25 or less for at least 20 minutes after waking) with its Body Battery change.
- Reading: a deterministic headline from sleep against the 14-night mean and the presence of a high-stress episode.

When garmin_mcp cannot provide the day, the page says why (unavailable, reconnection required, rate limit) and the 14-day scale stays available.

## Records

`/progress/records` reads the complete activity and lap history, 400 days of observations, elapsed time, distance and elevation arrays from normalized Garmin tracks (validated with Zod, at most 240 samples), Garmin predictions, the Garmin lactate threshold and the profile threshold heart-rate override. Read errors produce an explicit failure state.

- Reading and À portée: the road distance whose Garmin prediction is closest to its record, within 3 %, with "Préparer une tentative avec le coach". Without one, the reading counts documented records. The goal race card summarizes its preparation.
- Tes repères actuels: Garmin threshold pace, threshold heart rate (the profile value wins over Garmin), VO₂ max with its change against the latest value at least 90 days older, and the 20-minute VAM record. The zones link opens the "Issu de ton entraînement" section of profile settings.
- Vitesse: 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon from outdoor road and track runs only. Whole activities or adjacent valid laps must cover the distance with at most 5 % overshoot; duration scales to the distance; ties keep the earliest day, then activity identifier. A record set in the last 30 days that improves a previous one shows its gain and "nouveau"; otherwise the row shows the Garmin prediction.
- Endurance: longest outdoor run, highest single-run gain (a measured zero is valid), best 20-minute VAM (1,200 s windows averaging 12–16 % with no descent or sample gap above 300 s, gain × 3; whole-run gain never substitutes) and the biggest Monday week of running distance with its D+ when every run that week has elevation.

Activities and observations dated after today are excluded. These are Stride estimates from Garmin data, not official Garmin records. Each row opens its source activity.
