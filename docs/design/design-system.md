# Design system direction

Stride should feel modern, premium, technical, athletic, calm, and data-oriented. The current page is a small foundation shell, not a product dashboard.

## Foundations

- Typography pairs a clear sans-serif for reading with a restrained monospace for compact metadata.
- Spacing uses a consistent rhythm and generous separation around primary information.
- Surfaces should form a small hierarchy through background and borders before adding shadows.
- Use a compact radius scale; reserve stronger rounding for controls and meaningful emphasis.
- Keep motion short and purposeful. It should communicate changes in state or hierarchy and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Present metrics with units, period, and context. Avoid implying precision beyond the data.
- Charts should prioritize legibility, accessible labels, and clear time or distance scales.

The palette and landing shell are provisional. shadcn/ui may supply low-level accessible primitives when a real interaction needs them; it is not a visual system by itself.
