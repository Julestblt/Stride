# Product principles

## Coach the whole athlete

Training guidance should account for goals, history, recovery, terrain, schedule, and athlete preferences rather than treating each workout in isolation.

## Make reasoning legible

Recommendations should identify the relevant context and uncertainty. The system should not present estimates as measured facts.

## Respect athlete agency

Advice supports an athlete's decisions. It does not replace medical care or imply that one plan fits every body.

## Road and trail belong together

The product should support road pace and race structure alongside elevation, surface, terrain, and course demands.

## Prefer useful calm

Present a small number of clear, actionable signals. Use motion and visual emphasis to explain state, not to decorate.
