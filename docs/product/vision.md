# Product vision

Stride is a personal running coach that helps road and trail athletes make better training decisions over time. It should connect the athlete's goals, training history, current readiness, activities, and race demands into guidance that is useful and explainable.

## Long-term capabilities

- Personalized training plans for road and trail
- Race preparation and actionable race strategy
- Activity history, performance trends, and training load
- Recovery and readiness analysis
- Garmin activity and health data ingestion
- AI-supported plan adjustment and conversational coaching

These are product directions, not claims about capabilities in the current application.

## Product references

The [AI Running Coach project](https://github.com/mmornati/ai-running-coach) is a conceptual reference for connecting athlete context, specialized coaching roles, reusable running skills, recovery, and race preparation. Stride adopts those product ideas as design input and does not copy its implementation.

## Coaching model

Stride combines software, reliable athlete data, and specialized coaching reasoning. The coach should respect the athlete's context, distinguish evidence from inference, explain consequential recommendations, and adapt conservatively when data is incomplete.
