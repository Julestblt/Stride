# Product roadmap

This roadmap captures sequencing, not delivery promises. GitHub Issues are the source of actionable scope.

## Foundation

- Establish the web application, engineering workflow, documentation, and validation.
- Confirm product discovery priorities and athlete data boundaries.

## Athlete context and data

- Define athlete identity, profile, consent, and data-retention expectations.
- Design normalized activity and health data contracts.
- Build Garmin ingestion through a dedicated integration service.

## Coaching capabilities

- Establish training and recovery domain models with tested calculations.
- Add plan generation and adjustment behind explicit AI boundaries.
- Add race preparation, performance insights, and conversational coaching.

## Quality and trust

- Evaluate coaching behavior against curated cases.
- Monitor data freshness, failed syncs, and AI output quality.
- Expand accessibility, privacy, and operational readiness before broad release.
